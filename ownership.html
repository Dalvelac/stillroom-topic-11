IDOR in orders and receipts

Who can read an order, and who can download the receipt attached to it?

Catalogue searchReflected search textOrder and receipt ownershipFulfilment permissions

Step by step

  1. Sign in as Alex, add two bags of Roaster’s Reserve to the cart and approve the simulated checkout. The fresh fixture created SR-2105 at €48.00. The order detail page displayed the download link.
  2. Download the receipt from that page. The browser verified the filename stillroom-receipt-6105.html and the attachment contained both R-6105 and SR-2105. After signing in separately as Jamie, My orders listed SR-2104 and SR-2102.
  3. Read the two ownership queries. Customer order reads match the authenticated customer ID. Receipt reads match their own resource ID and customer ID. A composite foreign key associates a receipt with the correct order owner.
  4. Run both focused regressions. They use independent guest, owner, peer and staff sessions on fixtures. Read the response-body and attachment assertions alongside the status matrix below.
