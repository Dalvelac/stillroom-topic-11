POST /staff/orders/{id}/status

Business logic in fulfilment

Can only staff advance an order, and must they follow the permitted sequence?

Catalogue searchReflected search textOrder and receipt ownershipFulfilment permissions

Step by step

  1. Sign in as Sam, open SR-2105 from Fulfilment and choose Start processing. The allowed sequence is Placed, Processing, Shipped, Delivered. The initial action advanced the order by one stage.
  2. Sign in again as Alex and reopen SR-2105. The detail page now showed Processing and a two-event timeline. In the saved mitmdump flows, the status POST returned 303 and the following order GET returned 200.
  3. Run the permission regression. It holds the requested transition constant. A guest is redirected to sign in, the customer receives 403 and the persisted status stays Placed. Staff then succeeds on that same valid step.
  4. Run the sequence regression. It holds the staff identity constant and checks skipped, repeated, backward and terminal updates. Invalid changes return 409. The test reads the ordered event history after the permitted changes.
Result for this fixtureThe role check and transition check both held in their respective regressions. The customer’s fresh read and the stored event sequence connect the successful staff action to a committed change.

A redirect alone cannot establish that the database changed. A successful staff update covers one valid path. The distinct permission and transition tests check the rejected paths.
